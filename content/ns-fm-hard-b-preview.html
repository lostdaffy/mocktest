  2. a + b
  3. a × b
  4. It depends on a and b / यह a और b पर निर्भर है

Solution: A prime dividing ab divides a or b - say a. If it also divided a + b it would divide b, but a and b share no prime. So no prime divides both a + b and ab: the HCF is 1 (for example, 4 + 9 = 13 and 4 × 9 = 36).
